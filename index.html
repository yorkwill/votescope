Methodology & sources

It polls every 60 seconds while this page remains open and displays the latest data available from upstream publishers. New candidate totals may arrive less frequently. Replay and Pulse use this browser’s own saved observations; they do not reconstruct earlier returns. Votes counted, precincts reporting, estimated votes counted, and winners called are different measures. A leader is not a winner. Missing states in the official-state feed mean no feed coverage, not zero votes. A race is counted as called only when the results provider identifies a named race-calling source.

Sources: The People's Tally (CC BY 4.0, public results snapshot; original source on each race); Open America (official state election office feeds, currently covers 22 states; no projections). AP elections for a separate national race-call reference. Historical data: Election Wrld (CC BY 4.0) using MIT Election Data and Science Lab (CC0). 2026 boundary differences are flagged conservatively until verified for an individual district. Source coverage is not exhaustive.
